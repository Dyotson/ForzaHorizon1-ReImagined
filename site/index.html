Forza Horizon 1ReImagined

The 2012 open-world racer, running natively on Linux and Windows, rebuilt around the modern Horizon experience: a shared online Colorado, leaderboards and rivals, and 60, 120 or 144 frames per second.

Mile 12 · The mountain roads

What you get

  • Better gameplay. 60, 120 or 144 fps with physics and race timing intact, native controllers, saves and all DLC.
  • Online free roam. The Horizon World playlist puts every online player into one shared Colorado, where events fill up with players and free grid slots with AI.
  • Leaderboards and rivals. Times, ghosts and bounties shared between players, Monthly Rivals, car clubs and the Message Center, all live again.
  • Your own servers. Xbox LIVE and Turn 10's services are gone, so the project ships the Forza Live server, self-hostable, with a public instance to join.

Early work in progress, tested mostly in short sessions. Races with many AI cars are GPU-bound.

Mile 27 · The open highway

Install in four steps

  1. Get the installer. Take the Windows installer or the Linux package from the latest release. It installs the launcher; on Windows it also sets up the Visual C++ 2015-2022 runtime.
  2. Point it at your game. In the launcher's Setup page choose your own Forza Horizon Games on Demand package (the base version), and optionally the Title Update and your DLC folder. They are extracted for you.
  3. Build. The launcher generates the game from your copy and compiles it on your computer, downloading a compiler if you do not have one. It takes about 5 minutes on a fast 8-core desktop and longer on slower machines.
  4. Play. Pick your frame rate, controls and online server in Settings, and press Play.

You will need

  • Linux x86-64 or Windows 10/11 64-bit, with a Vulkan 1.3 GPU
  • About 8 GB of free RAM and 20 GB of disk space
  • Your own copy of Forza Horizon for Xbox 360
  • A controller is recommended (keyboard and mouse is untested)

The Linux version is the most tested. Windows has run under Proton; native Windows testing is still ongoing.

The download contains no game code and no game data. The installer never ships a built game: your copy is the only source of the game on your PC.

Mile 41 · Playing online

One shared Colorado

The game signs in to a Forza Live server as a Gold member. Choose in the launcher how you play:

Public server
The shared world, leaderboards and rivals. The address is dyotserver.ddns.net:21200, filled in for you. You forward no ports.
Your own server
Run server/forza_live_server.py where your friends can reach it. It needs only Python 3. Hosting guide
This PC or offline
A private local server for yourself, or no Xbox LIVE at all.

In Multiplayer, pick Find Game and the Horizon World playlist: the server drops you into the fullest open world, and everyone who joins after you is placed with you.
