Mile 0 · The festival

Forza Horizon 1ReImagined

The 2012 open-world racer, running natively on Windows and Linux. Single player through the festival, online free roam, leaderboards and rivals. Windows is what most players use now. The frame rate is yours to set: 30 fps is the original game, and the right choice on a Steam Deck or a low-end PC.

Mile 12 · The mountain roads

What you get

  • The whole festival, single player. Free roam, festival events and races, showcase events, audio and saves have been played through on ReImagined. A Xenia Canary save can be continued.
  • Title Update 4. The default when you supply it. Horizon Rally (the co-driver is heard) and the 1000 Club, and all 23 DLC packages can be installed.
  • Keyboard, gamepad, or a wheel. Keyboard and mouse cover menus, free roam, races, the map and photo mode. Throttle and brake on the keyboard are on/off. Racing wheels use the game's force feedback, and can use an H-shifter and a clutch. Logitech G29, G920 and G923 are tested. A DualSense can use adaptive triggers (resistance as you press; off by default).
  • Picture. AMD FSR 1, AMD FSR 3 and CAS, plus SMAA. On Windows, an RTX GPU can use NVIDIA DLSS and DLAA. Render scale, a 540p scene option for weak laptops, world detail (shadows and distant textures), scene anti-aliasing (MSAA) options, HDR on Vulkan, and ultrawide gameplay (menus and the map stay 16:9). Frame-rate presets include Match display.
  • Photos, the minimap, saves. A photo gallery in the launcher, faster radio station changes as an option, sharper minimap icons, automatic save backups, a profile editor, and the seven unicorn cars if your save is missing them. After a crash, Play can show a report. Nothing is uploaded unless you send it.
  • Online free roam. Horizon World places you in a shared Colorado of up to 8 players, with drop-in while a world is running. Friends, chat, leaderboards, Rivals, car clubs and the Message Center go through the Forza Live server.
  • Co-op Festival. A multiplayer playlist for friends: the world's host types /event in chat, everyone races that festival event, and every player's career advances. A loaner car is lent if you have none that fits.
  • Mods and HD texture packs. A Mods page and a Texture packs page in the launcher. Add a pack or a mod from a .zip, turn each on or off, and set the order.
  • Your own server, or the public one. Xbox LIVE and Turn 10's services are gone. The project ships Forza Live. You can host it, or join the public server. You forward no ports for the public one.

On a Steam Deck or a low-end PC, leave the frame rate at 30. The high numbers in the next section are from one desktop.

Mile 18 · Make it yours

Together, modded, sharper

Co-op Festival
Pick it under Multiplayer, Find Game. The host types /event FR05 (or any event code; /events lists them) and everyone races that festival event with CPU drivers. After the session the launcher adds each player's result, XP and prize to their own save. Design notes
Mods
The launcher's Mods page: Add mod... takes a .zip or a folder. Turn mods on or off and put them in order; the one lower in the list wins when two change the same file. Mods survive rebuilds and updates. Modding guide
HD texture packs
Install a community pack, or press Make HD pack to upscale your own textures on your PC with a local AI upscaler (Real-ESRGAN). Packs are compressed, about 4x less graphics memory. Vulkan only. Nothing of the game's art is shared. Texture packs guide
Leaderboards and map
The public server's speed traps, speed zones, Rivals and event times on a map of Colorado. Open the leaderboards
Steam Deck
Install with the Flatpak, use the Deck preset, and keep the frame rate low. Steam Deck guide

Mile 36 · The open highway

Install with the launcher

The normal way to play. It updates itself (from v0.1.9 on) and builds the game on your PC. Windows and Linux.

  1. Get the launcher. From the latest release: the Windows installer, the Linux Flatpak (recommended on SteamOS; flatpak install --user and the file, then once flatpak install flathub org.freedesktop.Sdk.Extension.llvm22//26.08 or it downloads a 2 GB compiler), or the Linux .tar.gz.
  2. Game files. In Setup, point at your own copy: a disc image, a Games on Demand package, or an extracted folder. Under Updates & DLC, add Title Update 4 and your DLC folder if you have them.
  3. Build. The launcher generates the game from your copy and compiles it, downloading a compiler if you do not have one. One measured build at 4 jobs on an 8-core PC took about 4.6 minutes. It can be cancelled and resumed.
  4. Prebuild shaders, then play. Prebuild shaders is optional and Vulkan only. It compiles pipelines from your files before you drive. Then Play. Settings cover the frame rate (including Match display), picture, controls, the online server and your profile. Saves backs up the profile for you.

You will need

  • Windows 10/11 64-bit, or Linux x86-64. Vulkan 1.3, or Direct3D 12 on Windows. Vulkan is the one to use if an NVIDIA card flickers.
  • About 4 GB of free RAM (the game peaks near 1.1 GB, a build near 2 GB at 4 jobs) and 15 GB of disk space
  • Your own copy of Forza Horizon for Xbox 360
  • A controller, a keyboard and mouse, or a wheel

Windows is what most players use now. Direct3D 12 and Vulkan both run there, through the Windows installer.

The download contains no game code and no game data. The installer never ships a built game: your copy is the only source of the game on your PC.

Mile 48 · Playing online

Horizon World

The game signs in to a Forza Live server as a Gold member. In Settings, under Online, choose how you play:

Public server
Horizon World, leaderboards and rivals. The address is forza-reimagined.playit.plus:56677, filled in for you. You forward no ports.
My own server
A Forza Live server you or a friend host. It needs only Python 3. Hosting guide
This PC
A private server for this session. Online features work, and only you are in it.
Offline
No Xbox LIVE and no server.

In Multiplayer, pick Find Game and Horizon World. The server puts you in the fullest open world, up to 8 players, or you open one and drive. Someone who joins later can jump in. Title Update 4 and base-game players are kept in separate rooms, since the two versions cannot play together. The Auction House has no screens in this game.
